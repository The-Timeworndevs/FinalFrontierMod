# Final Frontier
___
Final Frontier is an attempt at another space mod for Fabric with a different approach to the concept. Instead of purely focusing on the steps to get to the bodies, it focuses primarily about what you do when you *get* there, essentially applying the new Minecraft philosophy to a space mod.
___
## What does this mod do?
___
Final Frontier aims to add numerous planets and moons to visit, each with its own unique atmosphere (sometimes literally) and theme, adding on to the base experience. On top of that, Final Frontier aims to add on to the vanilla progression rather than being an alternative route like most other mods. In order to get certain materials for the project, you need to have already collected Netherite Scrap, and it is recommended to have at least diamond-tier armor, albeit upgraded to allow you to explore the hostile atmospheres of most worlds.
___
## Content
___
As of current we have the following:

### The Moon: (Incomplete as of current)
* A world mostly lifeless, you'll often encounter dangerous undead here, save for some remnants from the age of the Old Builders. 
* You'll rarely find lunar villages populated by ordinary villagers that have their own space suits.
* Upgraded illagers (some cybernetically augmented) resenting the disappearance of the former players before your time...
* The Lunar Vaults, a Trial Chambers like structure that can give epic, space-themed loot!
* Decompressed, zombie astronauts that roam the airless world, capable of sapping oxygen from your tanks.
* Sharpshooters, the skeletons of the Moon, they are equipped with plasma blasters, their projectiles do not fall unlike arrows
